## What is this?
Sthira is a student project. It is a browser app that uses a camera to follow 33 points on the body, such as the shoulders, hips and knees. It aims to help a physiotherapist time and count three standard tests from the US CDC: the 30-second chair stand, the 4-stage balance test and the Timed Up-and-Go. We are recording volunteers so we can tune and test the app.

**This is not a medical assessment. No result from this session is diagnostic, and we will not tell you anything about your health or your risk of falling.**

## What you will do
A short session of light activity, with a team member (the spotter) beside you the whole time:
- sit still for 5 seconds, then stand still for 5 seconds;
- stand up and sit down from a chair for 30 seconds, twice: once in loose clothing (saree, dhoti, lungi or similar) and once in track-pants;
- a few stands where we ask you to stop halfway or to use your hands;
- stand in 4 positions for up to 10 seconds each, from feet together to standing on one foot;
- twice, stand up, walk 3 metres, turn, walk back and sit down.

## Risks
Standing and balance tasks carry a small risk of losing your balance or falling. To reduce it, a spotter stays within arm's reach, you can skip any task, and we stop at once if you feel dizzy, unwell or in pain. Please do not take part if you have been told to avoid this kind of activity, have had a recent fall, injury or surgery to your legs or back, or feel unwell today.

## What we record and who sees it
- **Body landmark coordinates and timestamps:** numbers for the position of each body point over time. They are stored with your participant code, age band and clothing type. No name.
- **Video:** kept only on the team's recording laptop. It is seen only by the team and one outside labeller (ideally a physiotherapist), who marks each movement. It is not uploaded or shared. It is deleted after labelling, by ______ (date).
- Your signed form is kept on paper, separate from the data. It is the only place your name and code appear together.
- We will not publish the landmark data as a dataset.
