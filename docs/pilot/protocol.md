# Sthira pilot recording protocol (v1, 23 Oct 2026)

One page for the students running sessions. Read it once before the first participant. Use participant codes only (any code you choose, for example P01, P02 or A11: letters, digits, - and _). Never write a name in any file, file name or log.

## Equipment
- Recording laptop with the Sthira app open in record mode (or a phone, if that is what the demo will use). Charger plugged in.
- Stand or stack of books to hold the device still. Do not hand-hold.
- Chair with a straight back and no armrests. CDC uses a seat 17 inches (about 43 cm) high. Measure our chair and write the seat height in the log.
- For TUG, use a standard armchair if you have one (the CDC TUG sheet says "standard arm chair"). If not, use the same chair and write "no armchair" in the log.
- Tape measure, stopwatch (a second phone is fine), masking tape.
- Mark a line on the floor 3 m from the front of the TUG chair with masking tape.
- Printed consent forms and this protocol. A pen.

## Camera placement (Sthira protocol v1)
| Decision | Reason |
|---|---|
| Camera at about 45 degrees to the person's front (halfway between front-on and side-on). If the room does not allow it, use side-on and write that in the log. | Side-on shows the hips and knees straighten. 45 degrees also shows the hands on the chest. Sit2Stand used 45 degrees. |
| Whole body in frame, head to feet, with the chair, in both sitting and standing. Check this before every trial. | Calibration and the quality gates need every landmark visible. Cut-off feet or head make the count unreliable. |
| Measure camera height (floor to lens) and distance (lens to chair front) every session. Do not move the camera during a session. | We do not fix these numbers yet. Writing them down lets us explain failures and set up the venue the same way. |
| For TUG, move the camera back if needed so the chair and the 3 m line are both in frame. Measure again. | The person must stay in frame for the whole walk. |
| Plain background, light from the front or side, not from behind the person. | Backlight and clutter lower landmark visibility. |

## Session order (one participant)
Start a new recording for each trial. Name it `<code>_<trial>`, for example `P03_cs-loose`.

1. **Consent.** Read the information sheet together. Sign. Tick boxes. Assign the next code.
2. **Calibration** (`cal`). Stand straight and still in front of the chair, arms crossed on the chest, looking ahead, for 5 s. Then sit still against the backrest, arms crossed, for 5 s. The test starts ("Go") from this seated position, as the CDC requires. If the app says calibration failed, redo it. Never skip it.
3. **30 s chair stand, loose clothing** (`cs-loose`): saree, dhoti, lungi or other loose clothing. Arms crossed at the wrists, hands on opposite shoulders. Say "Go" and start the stopwatch. Stop at 30 s. A second student counts full stands silently and writes the count in the log.
4. **30 s chair stand, track-pants** (`cs-track`). Same as step 3. Let the person change and rest first.
5. **3 deliberate half-stands** (`half`). "Rise partway, then sit back down without standing up fully." Rest a few seconds between each.
6. **3 arm-assisted stands** (`arm`). Do one of each: one hand leaves the chest, push on the thighs, push on the chair seat.
7. **4 balance stages** (`bal1` to `bal4`): feet side-by-side, semi-tandem, tandem, single leg. 10 s each. The spotter helps the person into position, lets go when they are steady, then the timer starts. Arms out to the side are allowed; moving the feet is not. If a stage is not held for 10 s, stop the balance test there (CDC rule).
8. **2 TUG trials** (`tug1`, `tug2`). Sit back against the backrest. On "Go", start the stopwatch: stand, walk at normal pace to the 3 m line, turn, walk back, sit down. Stop the stopwatch when they are seated. Write both times in the log. Normal footwear; walking aid allowed.

## Safety rules
- A spotter stands beside the person, within arm's reach, for every trial. The spotter does nothing else.
- Stop at once if the person reports dizziness, pain, breathlessness or says they want to stop. No questions asked.
- Skip all balance stages for anyone who feels unsteady or whom the spotter is unsure about. Write "balance skipped" in the log.
- Let the person rest whenever they ask. Offer water.
- Keep the floor clear and dry. No loose rugs. Clothing must not trail where it can be stepped on.
